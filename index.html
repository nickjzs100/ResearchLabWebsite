Research

From measured vibrations to informed action

The lab spans structural dynamics, smart structures, structural control, real-time hybrid simulation, optimal design, smart connected health, and innovative engineering education.

Structural Health Monitoring

Continuous sensing and analytics help evaluate bridge, building, and infrastructure performance before small anomalies become expensive failures.

  • Wireless sensing and data acquisition
  • Damage detection and diagnosis
  • Risk-informed decision support for civil infrastructure

Smart Structures

Smart structural systems combine sensing, actuation, and computation to adapt to loads, support inspections, and improve operational resilience.

  • Embedded sensor networks
  • Adaptive infrastructure concepts
  • Cyber-physical testing workflows

Structural Dynamics & Control

Dynamic response, structural control, and real-time hybrid simulation create a bridge between laboratory experiments and field-scale structural behavior.

  • Real-time hybrid simulation
  • Seismic response and structural control
  • Multi-directional motion and high-performance measurement

Smart Connected Health

Floor vibrations and home sensing can reveal gait parameters and health indicators while preserving everyday living patterns.

  • AI-assisted home health monitoring systems
  • At-home gait inference from floor vibrations
  • Interdisciplinary sensing and human-centered data analysis

Innovative Engineering Education

The lab develops equipment, platforms, and AI-supported learning environments that make engineering concepts tangible, collaborative, and student-centered.

  • Student-centered collaborative AI learning
  • Remote and online laboratory experiences
  • Research experiences for undergraduate engineers
